## Direction contract

THESIS: The visitor unwraps a florist’s parcel from a person they miss; the payoff is a living bouquet, not the animation of a button.

OWN-WORLD: Cream folded tissue printed with roses, ribbon in mulberry silk, filtered afternoon light across a blush table. Keep the familiar stationery type and ink.

STORY: Pull the ribbon or tap to untie it. Hear the fabric and paper give way. Watch a flower loosen and the rest answer. Look closer at the petals, turn the bouquet, or send the exact moment on.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A short centered invitation above one wrapped parcel. Sound is available before the first touch. The bow and note lead; the opening action is inside the gift, not in a toolbar.
